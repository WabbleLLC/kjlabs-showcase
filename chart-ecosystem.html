Step by step

  1. Inputs: CAD from Onshape, STEP or STL; field markups and site notes; or an AI design chat that becomes a 3D model.
  2. KJLabs Simulators: the FEA pipeline, 3D duct designer, airflow and loss simulator, and the fan and cyclone quick-look.
  3. Open-source solvers: four structural codes and two CFD codes run headless, each validated on a reference case first.
  4. CompliSpec: results and duct layouts are checked against requirements stored as code, one jurisdiction at a time.
  5. Three honest outcomes: a pass or fail with its source, an inspection checklist for procedural rules, or not computable with the missing inputs named.

Why it is built this way

Most engineering tools give an answer whether or not they have the inputs for it. This system has a third outcome on purpose. When a rule can't be checked from the data available, it says so and names what is missing. That makes it safe to use for compliance work.
